## 1. Summary of Changes Made

### A. Coupled Eye-to-Base Kinematics (`src_nodes/brain_node.py`)
* **Problem:** The chassis previously evaluated optical pixel offset alone ($e_x \approx 0$), ignoring the physical gimbal pan angle when the camera turned to follow the operator.
* **Implementation:**
  * Subscribed to `/servo_s1` (`std_msgs/Int32`) with freshness check ($< 0.5\,\text{s}$) and physical clamp ($\pm 60^\circ$).
  * Coupled optical error with normalized gimbal pan angle ($\text{gimbal\_norm} = \frac{\theta_{\text{pan}}}{65.0}$):
    $$\text{total\_error} = \text{gimbal\_norm} + \text{optical\_error}$$
    $$\omega_z = -\text{total\_error} \cdot K_{p, \text{yaw}} \quad (K_{p, \text{yaw}} = 1.5)$$
  * Deadband: $\pm 4\%$ of frame ($\pm 0.04$).
  * Result: When the camera head glances left, the wheels immediately pivot left until the head realigns with the body.

### B. 3-Stage Graceful Re-Acquisition FSM (`src_nodes/brain_node.py`)
* **Problem:** A rigid 3.0s hard drop previously aborted the mission and erased `current_gesture` if the operator momentarily turned their back or walked past an obstacle.
* **Implementation:**
  * **Stage 1 ($0 \le t \le 2.0\,\text{s}$):** Coast forward speed smoothly to $0.0\,\text{m/s}$ while holding `current_gesture = GESTURE_FOLLOW` in active memory and continuing to steer toward the last known bearing.
  * **Stage 2 ($2.0 < t \le 5.0\,\text{s}$):** Chassis stays parked; system maintains active search window. If the operator reappears within 5 seconds, tracking resumes instantly without requiring a new gesture.
  * **Stage 3 ($t > 5.0\,\text{s}$):** Safe unlock and transition to `PARKED`.

### C. Rectangular Cartesian Corridor LiDAR Safety (`src_nodes/brain_node.py`)
* **Problem:** The polar $\pm 45^\circ$ cone expanded to a $2.0\,\text{m}$ width at $1.0\,\text{m}$ range, falsely flagging side tables and stools as frontal collision hazards.
* **Implementation:**
  * Converted polar points to robot Cartesian frame ($x = r \cos \theta$, $y = r \sin \theta$).
  * Clipped detection to the vehicle driving corridor:
    $$|y| \le \frac{w_{\text{chassis}}}{2} + \delta_y = 0.18\,\text{m} \quad (\text{Width } = 36\,\text{cm})$$
  * True frontal hazards ($0.05 \le x < 0.36\,\text{m}$) within the corridor trigger emergency halt, active 20cm reverse retreat, and acoustic alarm (`/beep`). Lateral obstacles ($|y| > 0.18\,\text{m}$) are safely bypassed.

### D. Two-Tiered Battery Protection (`src_nodes/brain_node.py`)
* **Problem:** Battery discharge below $6.8\,\text{V}$ caused the factory motor expansion board to cut power, resulting in brownout motor chatter.
* **Implementation:**
  * Subscribed to `/battery` (`std_msgs/UInt16`).
  * **Tier 1 ($< 7.0\,\text{V}$):** Periodic acoustic warning pulse on `/beep` (throttled every 10s) and dashboard amber alert.
  * **Tier 2 ($< 6.8\,\text{V}$):** Proactive software auto-park (`cmd_vel = 0.0`) to cleanly lock out motor commands before entering hardware brownout.

### E. Pre-Flight Autonomy Joy Suppression (`scripts/launch_autonomy.sh` & `scripts/stop_autonomy.sh`)
* **Problem:** Factory `yahboom_joy` background daemon continuously spammed `Twist(0, 0)` at 20 Hz, causing DDS topic contention and motor jitter during autonomy.
* **Implementation:**
  * In `launch_autonomy.sh`: Automatically pauses `ChassisServer` in `yahboom_base` before starting autonomy nodes.
  * Created `scripts/stop_autonomy.sh`: Automatically restarts `ChassisServer` when autonomy stops, guaranteeing that factory gamepad driving is immediately restored.

## 3. Bidirectional Corridor Safety & Rear Collision Guard (`src_nodes/brain_node.py`)

* **Safety Flaw Identified:** When a frontal obstacle triggered the 20 cm reactive reverse at $-0.12\,\text{m/s}$, the robot previously reversed blindly without evaluating obstacles behind it.
* **Algorithmic Solution:**
  * Expanded the Cartesian corridor to evaluate rear points ($-0.30 \le x \le -0.05\,\text{m}$, $|y| \le 0.18\,\text{m}$).
  * **Pre-Reverse Check:** If rear clearance is $< 0.25\,\text{m}$, reactive reverse is prohibited; chassis halts immediately ($v_x = 0.0, \omega_z = 0.0$) with an acoustic alarm pulse.
  * **In-Flight Obstacle Interruption:** If an obstacle enters the rear path during reverse, the maneuver is aborted instantaneously.
  * **`GESTURE_BACK` Gating:** Backward velocity is clamped to $0.0\,\text{m/s}$ whenever the rear is obstructed.

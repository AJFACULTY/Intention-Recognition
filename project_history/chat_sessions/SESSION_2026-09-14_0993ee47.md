### 2. Can We Make the Sweep Much Smoother?

**Yes, significantly smoother.** There were four specific control factors causing the slight micro-step jitter:

1. **Search Slew-Rate Jump (Fixed):**  
   During search mode, the node had an override allowing up to $2.0^\circ$ of travel per tick ($40^\circ/\text{s}$), which caused noticeable stepped jumps. We have clamped the search slew rate to **$0.6^\circ/\text{tick}$** ($18^\circ/\text{s}$), turning the movement into a smooth, cinematic glide.
2. **Control Loop Frequency ($20\,\text{Hz} \to 30\,\text{Hz}$):**  
   Increasing the update rate from $20\,\text{Hz}$ to **$30\,\text{Hz}$** divides the motion into finer sub-degree increments, eliminating visible step intervals.
3. **Serial Bus De-Duplication (UART Traffic Optimization):**  
   Previously, the node published integer packets 20 times every second even when the rounded angle had not changed. We updated `publish_servos` in [`src_nodes/active_vision_node.py`](file:///home/j/ros2_cognition_ws/src_nodes/active_vision_node.py) to **only send commands when the angle actually changes**, keeping the micro-ROS serial bridge on `/dev/ttyUSB0` completely free of packet clustering.
4. **Cinematic Period Tuning ($0.08\,\text{Hz}$, $\pm 25^\circ$):**  
   We softened the turnaround amplitude to $\pm 25^\circ$ with a slightly longer $12.5\text{-second}$ period, eliminating abrupt deceleration at the sinusoidal extremes.

### 1. What Prevents False Positives From the Gimbal? (The Closed-Loop Cross-Check) 🎯

This is a classic question in control engineering: *What happens if the gimbal reports an angle, but physically it stalled, slipped, or didn't move?*

Because the camera is physically mounted **on the gimbal itself**, the camera image and the servo angle are optically locked together:

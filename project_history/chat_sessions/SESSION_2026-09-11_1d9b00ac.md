### 1. The `FOLLOW` Gesture Logic Deep-Dive

Currently in [brain_node.py](file:///home/j/ros2_cognition_ws/src_nodes/brain_node.py#L268-L285), `FOLLOW` mode relies strictly on the camera image bounding box:
- Angular steering: $\omega = -1.5 \times (x_{\text{center}} - 0.5)$
- Linear drive: $v = 0.20\,\text{m/s}$ if bounding box width $w < 0.45$, and $0.0\,\text{m/s}$ if $w \ge 0.45$.

While functional on a bench with a fixed camera, **on the mobile robot with an active 2-DOF gimbal, this creates the "Kinematic Decoupling" problem** (documented in Chapter 5 of the thesis):
1. When the operator walks to the right, the **gimbal servos immediately pan right** to keep the operator centered in the video frame.
2. Because the operator is centered in the camera frame, $x_{\text{center}} \approx 0.5$, meaning **the image error is zero**!
3. The chassis thinks the human is directly ahead, so the wheels drive straight forward while the camera is craned $35^\circ$ to the right.

```
       [Operator]
          ▲
           \  Gimbal Pan Angle (φ)
            \ 
          [Gimbal] (Centers image: x_error ≈ 0)
             |
         [Chassis] ---> Drives straight forward! (Decoupled)
```

#### Proposed Solution: Coordinated Gaze-and-Chassis Steering
Borrowing from biological gaze coordination (how a human's eyes/neck turn first, followed by the torso):
1. **Total Heading Error ($\theta_e$):**
   $$\theta_e = \phi_{\text{gimbal}} + \Delta\theta_{\text{image}}$$
   where $\phi_{\text{gimbal}}$ is the current pan servo angle ($[-60^\circ, +60^\circ]$ from `/servo_s1`) and $\Delta\theta_{\text{image}} \approx K_c \cdot (x_{\text{center}} - 0.5)$.
2. **Coordinated Chassis Steering:**
   $$\omega_{\text{chassis}} = -K_p \cdot \theta_e$$

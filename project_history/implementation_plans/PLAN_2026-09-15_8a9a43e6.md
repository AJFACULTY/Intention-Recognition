### Component 2: Physical Robot Test Protocol (While Charging)

#### Practical Punch List & Operational Procedure:
- **Pre-Flight Sanity Checks (While Battery Charges to $\ge 8.0\,\text{V}$):**
  1. Verify workspace synchronization (`./scripts/sync_to_bot.sh`).
  2. Confirm ONNX models and weights exist in container (`/root/cognition_ws/models/path_predictor.onnx`, `gesture_model.onnx`).
  3. Validate ROS 2 Domain ID (`ROS_DOMAIN_ID=20`) and RMW middleware (`rmw_fastrtps_cpp`).
- **Live Test Scenario Execution (Mission Patrol $\to$ Human Approach $\to$ Path Prediction $\to$ Path Blockage $\to$ Gesture Interaction):**
  1. Launch base navigation & AMCL localization: `bash ~/start_nav2.sh`.
  2. Dispatch patrol mission: `python3 ~/cognition_ws/mission_manager.py --mission UNATTENDED_FACILITY_PATROL`.
  3. Launch cognition & vision stack: `bash ~/start_bench_pipeline.sh`.
  4. Human approaches the robot along its path:
     - `person_detection_node` detects human centroid and feeds historical coordinates to `path_predictor.onnx`.
     - Trajectory vector (Approaching / Moving Away / Lateral) publishes to `/cognition/detection`.
  5. Human blocks corridor:
     - Frontal LiDAR (<0.36m safety corridor) triggers active emergency halt or Nav2 local costmap obstacle inflation, stopping vehicle smoothly without contact.
  6. Human issues gesture while robot is halted:
     - Human presents `STOP` (open palm) or `FOLLOW` (peace sign) or `GO` (thumbs up).
     - `brain_node` validates gesture via rolling 5-frame buffer, claims priority on `twist_mux`, and transitions state machine.

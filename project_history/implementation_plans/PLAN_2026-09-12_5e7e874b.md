## Articulated Robotics ([Josh Newans](https://www.youtube.com/@ArticulatedRobotics)) Architecture Alignment
In Josh Newans' Nav2 series (*"Making robot navigation easy with Nav2 and ROS!"*), the complete autonomous workflow consists of:
1. **Interactive 2D Goal Pose (Click-to-Goal):** Clicking anywhere on the map dynamically plans and dispatches a path to that spot.
2. **Visual Path Feedback:** Displaying the Global Plan (green line from robot to goal) and Local Controller Trajectory (cyan line) dodging obstacles.
3. **Mission Execution from a Mission Pool:** Dispatched patrols across predefined waypoints with safe inflation margins.
4. **Interactive Mission Dashboard:** We will bring this exact RViz-level interactive experience into our browser dashboard (`http://10.27.122.136:8080`), allowing you to dispatch missions or click anywhere on the room map from your phone or PC.
